What is where, and how to drive it

Route, leftThe numbers down the left edge are the steps; a dot under one is a question you asked there. Click a number to go there; hover the edge and the full list slides out over the page. Grey ones are still being written. The button at the top left pins the list open.
DiagramThe shape: who calls whom, where data goes. Click a box to ask for the code behind it or how it fits. With a project overview, each stop starts on the overview, zoomed to the box it lives in, then shows its own diagram; Overview / This stop over the pane, or O, flips between them.
CodeThe whole file, scrolled to the stop. Orange lines are the ones under discussion; the bar in the gutter marks the stop's range. Back to lines scrolls back to it after you have scrolled away.
SplittersDrag the bar between the panes, or the one above the narration. Split / Diagram / Code, bottom right, picks a layout. The route list opens over the panes, so it never resizes them.
speakerMute or unmute. Unmuted, every step speaks when it is shown and the tour moves on when the voice ends. The arrow beside it starts again from step 1.
gearSettings: whether the overview zooms out and in between stops, or follows the browser's reduced-motion setting.
← →Back, and forward. On an answer, forward returns to the step you asked from.
Space RPause or resume (in Export, Space ticks); say it again
L M HNewest step; mute; ask the agent to hold
[ ]Voice slower, faster (the − 1× + control at the top; click 1× to reset)
Typing a questionPins the step you are on: the tour waits until you send it or leave the box
PauseStays paused while you move between pages and read; Play speaks the page you are on and the tour runs again
wheel, dragZoom the diagram to the cursor, pan. Fit, 0 or a double-click shows the whole diagram. + − also work
OFlip the diagram pane between the project overview and the stop's own diagram
? EscThis panel; close things
Review / Export, top rightReview plays the tour. Export turns the route into the list an export is made from: every stop and every answer, each answer under the stop you asked it at. Only ticked sections go into the export: Tick all then untick what you do not want, or Untick all and tick the ones you do. An unticked section stays where it is, dimmed; a new one arrives ticked. Drag to reorder. Questions still work, and their answers join the list. In Export the voice plays only when you press Play. E switches.
Export…A Markdown record of the tour: the writing, every diagram as Mermaid, and only the highlighted lines of each stop's code. Download saves it from the browser and keeps a copy in ~/.local/share/cosight/exports.
Alt+↑ ↓ SpaceIn Export: move the section on screen up or down; tick or untick it (Space does not pause there; the Play button does)
